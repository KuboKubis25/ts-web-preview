Specialised Service designed specifically for Employers and Operators of Technology

## Employer/operator package

Operation package of services for employer/operator focuses on protecting the operator by reducing the exposure to legal risks, especially at the early stages of installation and beginning of the production. The cost-effective package includes following services:

- pre-handover inspection and validation of technology
- establishing of the technology in local legislation
- providing required professional qualifications to employee
- elaboration of mandatory safety documentation
- initial and periodical inspections and testing

Every employer is legally required to have the points above sorted in a satisfactory way. We offer the whole package as the most cost-effective solution for operators. With increasing number of services bundled together, our operational load becomes lower, due to the fact that we oversee the whole process (designs, documentation, suppliers, relevant institutions, etc.) for certain technology, which results in a significantly better price.

In addition, we also provide operators with a complex testing before commissioning. This exhaustive examinations ensures that your technology is ready for commissioning and all local legal requirements are fulfilled. The procedure includes testing of restricted technical devices, testing of environmental factors and fire-protection safety.

## Red Star programme

This is our premium service for operators of technology. On a period basis, we ensure that the technology is 100% compliant with local laws and norms, including the suggested solutions from the local norms. With this product, we guarantee that every legal discrepancy is identified and every local legal requirement is fulfilled when the technology is operated to Czech or Slovak Republic and no further claims can be made regarding the required legal conditions. Furthermore we guarantee the correctness of all legally mandatory documentation and evaluation of all locally required tests.

This service is offered for periods of the time and includes periodical deep safety/operational audit according to local legislation, thus making sure that not only are all of the periodic revisions in order, but also that the all of the operational manuals are correctly made and any hidden deficiencies are identified and fixed when discovered. It is unacceptable to find the same fault several times in a row in period revision reports.

Ensure the compatibility of your technology with local Czech and Slovak legislation through our Validation Red Star programme.


Our service specifically designed for suppliers shipping products to Slovak or Czech Republic

## Supplier package

Operation package of services for suppliers focuses on ensuring that the technology supplied to Czech and Slovak Republic adheres to local legislative standards in addition to standard CE certification. This translates to significantly more seamless sale to the operators and added value of your technology. The full package includes following services.

- pre-deliver inspection and validation of technology

- ensure that your product or technology is in accordance to local legislation and to add value to your clients
- establishing of the technology in local legislation

- ship your technology with inspection and revision schedule already prepared
- elaboration of mandatory safety documentation

- add value to your tech by providing locally sufficient safety manuals

This package allows you to ship the technology with all of the required local documentation ready at hand with Testing Service mark of Validation – Valid CZ, Valid SK.

The validation is awarded after only if no faults endangering the potential safety of operators are discovered. The validation of your technology then results in an increased confidence, trustworthiness and much longer-term relationship from your buyers.
