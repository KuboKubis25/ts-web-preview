We have required accreditations to provide these inspections in order to meet the legal needs of the technology.

Every RTD group has a specific (usually distinct) legal conditions for its period inspecting and testing. Restricted technical devices of category A (RTD-A) furthermore require to have design documentation approved before installation of the device or before reconstruction of the device.

Every employee who will be operating any restricted technical device (electric, pressure, gas or lifting) is legally required to have professional qualification to operate such device. In case of a reparation of such device, further official qualification is needed.

Testing Service provides the elaboration of all initial and periodic revisions, inspections and trainings of employees.

## Professional qualifications trainings

In addition to OSH (BOZP) training, we provide the training and education of professional competence of workers for operations and services of – Electric Devices and Technology – Pressure Devices and Technology – Gas Devices and Technology – Lifting Devices and Technology [See detailed list of training courses and accreditations here.](/s/TS-Opravnenie.pdf)

Establish your technology in local legislation

Establishing your technology in Slovak legislation consists of creating the periodic revision plan according to Slovak legislation along with list of exact professional qualifications that are needed in order to safely operate the technology. This input can be further used by education departments that will have sufficient data in order to be able ensure the right trainings for employees and operators of technology.

## Complex testing before commissioning

In addition, we provide a complex testing (including environmental, safety and fire protection) to ensure that technology is fulfilling all legal requirements and is ready for commissioning.In addition, we also provide operators with a complex testing before commissioning. This exhaustive examinations ensures that your technology is ready for commissioning and all local legal requirements are fulfilled. The procedure includes testing of restricted technical devices, testing of environmental factors and fire-protection safety.

With over 20 years of experience, we support our clients whether they need a revision, inspection, employee training and certification or complete establishment of a technology in local legislation.
