## Protect the safety of your employees by making sure that your technology is compliant with every local health and safety regulation.

According to current legislation, the employer is also responsible for identifying, reducing and removing of every potential risk related to workplace and technology. The risks must be eliminated in accordance with latest available science and technical findings.

### Do your safety standards comply with Slovak legislation?

Testing Service provides a third party analysis that identifies the safety risks of the employees and potential legal hazards. If required, this analysis can be used as a foundation for safety design fulfilling current legal conditions. In our project portfolio, we have developed complete safety designs and solutions in numerous large scale production facilities across Slovakia and Czechia.

### Make sure your required safety files are in order.

Employers are required to have elaborated numerous files in regard to the workplace and technology safety. - operational rules for restricted technical devices - electric, pressure, lifting an gas - protocol determining external influences - determining legally mandatory inspection periods of any technology - ATEX 137 must be elaborated by each employer if any area or technology poses a potential explosion risk

## These documents are important part of employers’ operational guidebook and are subject to an inspection. We offer elaboration and administration of any of the required documentations.

### Prevent the possible accidents by conducting an independent third-party analysis.

Our inspectors identify any unresolved risks and situations where potential workplace injury might occur. Every identified risk corresponds to a very negative potential consequence [What, When, How]. We search for these issues before they happen and design a solution to prevent them. Our aim is to offer an unbiased and independent inspection that will provide employers an objective view of the safety insufficiencies of their operation and mandatory safety documentation.

## There is no excuse in neglecting the safety of employees. Sadly, our experience suggests that often this is exactly what happens in order to simplify operational processes. We intend to determine where such neglects are happening in the process line and design a solution that is both perfectly safe and operationally reasonable.

### Three key steps

The whole process can be divided into three key steps. - Inspection of the workplace, technology, documentation and processes - Analysis of findings and determination of possible complications and neglects - Development of safety designs that offset the issues

Regular independent risk and safety assessment is the best way to avoid potential work related accidents and injuries. The usual result of such accidents are extrajudicial settlements, which result in heavy financial liability.

### Un-prevented workplace accidents and injuries

In case of occurred workplace injury and/or accident, we offer our consulting, inspection and analysis services to insurance companies, inspection offices and the police. We can also ensure legal support and analysis of unresolved and un-prevented risks involved in the accident on behalf of the stricken employee.

From our experience, this is often caused by a neglect of safety regulations to some degree by the employer.
