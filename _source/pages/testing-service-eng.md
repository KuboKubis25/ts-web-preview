# Our Services

 

 

### Permits & Approvals Assurance

Certain types of technology need a legal permit or approval even before they are installed, while all of them require a permit before operation and production.

Testing Service provides full administration of every legal requirement concerning installation and later production of your technology.

### Consulting Services - technical legislation and engineering standards 

The legislation in Czechia or Slovakia specifically states further legal conditions for production, commissioning and operation and subsequent revisions of any technical devices. In addition, there are numerous discrepancies between the EU and local technical norms and decrees in Slovak Republic and Czech Republic. 

### Risk Assessment, ATEX, PAWA

All employers and operators of technology and machinery are legally responsible for ensuring general workplace and operation safety.

We provide full assessment and analysis of machinery, technology and workplaces in order to minimise the risks and therefore ensuring the legal protection of the employer and health safety of the employees.

### Inspections, testing, certifications and professional qualifications

Slovak and Czech legislation requires the technology, machines and devices be subjected to both periodical and initial specialised inspections. We have required accreditations and to provide these inspections in order to meet the legal needs of the technology. In addition, we provide a complex testing (including environmental, safety and fire protection) to ensure that technology is fulfilling all legal requirements and is ready for commissioning.
