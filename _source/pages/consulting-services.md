### Ensuring you are paying for technology without any legal issues

Often, suppliers consider only the CE declaration as sufficient condition for their technology to operate anywhere in EU. However, in Slovak and Czech Republic this is not the case. Due to old STN/ČSN (Slovak and Czech Technical Norms), decrees, and national appendices to STN-EN norms, there are numerous additional requirements that must be passed in order for the technology to be compliant with local law. If subjected to inspection, technology with only CE can cause potential delays in production and/or various legal fees, depending on the severity of the deficiency.

Testing Service provides detailed, diligent check and inspection of fulfilment of CR/SR legal requirements and norms CSN, STN, EN for both technology (design) and the required documentation. The technology either passes the due diligence with TS approval stating that it adheres to local legislation or the inspection results in an output consisting of TS expert opinion detailing the insufficiencies that are required to be fixed before installation of the technology.

### Pre-deliver validation of the technology FOR SUPPLIERS OF TECHNOLOGY

Any new technology shipped to Czech or Slovak Republic or technology that is being reconstructed must be in accordance to local legislation and norms. Due to the differences between EU and CZ/SK norms and legislation, the CE certification of conformity is not enough to provide a full protection for the operator and further adjustments must be made to ensure that the machine/technology is locally compliant.
